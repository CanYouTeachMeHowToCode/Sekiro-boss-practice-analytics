# 内测须知 / Private test notice

The owner sends this to testers with the site address. The shared password goes separately; it is never written
here or anywhere in the repository. English below.

---

## 中文

感谢你参加内测！上传一段你打天守阁苇名弦一郎的录像，网站会找出你每一次扣血、被打崩和死亡，并告诉你是哪一招打中的。

### 怎么开始

1. 打开网址，先输入共享密码（用户名 `tester`，密码另外发给你）。
2. 注册你自己的账号。
3. 在「我的录像」里上传。

### 录像要求

- **只支持原版《只狼》的天守阁苇名弦一郎**，三个阶段都可以。不支持装了 mod 的游戏，也不支持再战里的「心中的弦一郎」和剑圣一心战开头的弦一郎。
- **游戏语言**：简体中文、繁體中文或英文。
- **分辨率**：16:9，建议 1080p 或更高。720p 也能分析，但更多失败会显示"招式不确定"。
- **帧率**：建议 60 帧。30 帧也能分析，但结果仅供参考，因为 30 帧的识别还在验证。录制软件要设成**固定帧率**（可变帧率的视频会被拒绝），不要开 HDR。
- 最长 10 分钟，最大 2 GB；MP4、MKV 或 MOV 格式。

### 怎么录，结果最准

- 从战斗一开始就录，录完整场。
- **开场尽早锁定 Boss**。没锁定的时候，系统看不清 Boss 出的是哪一招，这些失败会显示"招式不确定"。
- 不要裁剪画面，不要在画面上加摄像头或文字。

### 要等多久

只有一台机器，录像要排队，一段一段地分析：
- 上传后约 3 分钟，先检查格式和内容；
- 然后分析，时间约是录像长度的 8 到 9 倍。一段 3 分钟的录像大约要 25 分钟。
- 前面有人排队的话要等更久。页面会显示进度，不用一直开着，晚点回来看就行。

### 隐私

- 录像存在 Google Cloud 的美国机房。
- 你的录像和报告只有你自己和管理员（项目作者，为了排查问题）能看到，别的测试者看不到。
- 内测结束后，所有测试者的录像、片段和报告统一删除。
- 这是测试版，可能出错，数据也可能被重置。

### 反馈

最有帮助的是告诉我们：
- 哪一次失败的**招式不对**，或者**时间不对**；
- 有没有**漏掉**的失败，或者**多出来**的失败；
- 页面上哪里看不懂。

直接回复邀请你的人就行，写上录像名和失败的时间（比如"1:58.7 那次不是鹰射"）。

## English

Thanks for joining the private test! Upload a recording of your fight against Genichiro Ashina at the top of Ashina
Castle, and the site finds every time you lost HP, had your posture broken or died, and tells you which move hit you.

### Getting started

1. Open the site and enter the shared password first (user name `tester`; the password is sent to you separately).
2. Register your own account.
3. Upload under "Recordings" in the menu.

### What the recording must be

- **Unmodded Sekiro, Genichiro Ashina at the top of Ashina Castle**, any of his three phases. Modded games are not
  supported, and neither are the Reflection of Strength rematch nor the Genichiro who opens the Isshin fight.
- **Game language**: English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese.
- **Resolution**: 16:9, 1080p or higher recommended. 720p is analysed too, but more failures will show the move as
  unsure.
- **Frame rate**: 60 fps recommended. 30 fps is analysed too, but its results are for reference only while
  recognition at 30 fps is being validated. Set your recorder to a **constant frame rate** (variable frame rate videos
  are rejected), and turn HDR off.
- At most 10 minutes and 2 GB; MP4, MKV or MOV.

### How to record for the best results

- Start recording before the fight starts and record the whole fight.
- **Lock on to Genichiro as early as you can.** Without lock-on the system cannot see which move he used, and those
  failures show the move as unsure.
- Don't crop the video or put a webcam or text over the game.

### How long it takes

There is one machine, so recordings wait in line and are analysed one at a time:
- about 3 minutes after the upload, the format and content are checked;
- then the analysis takes about 8 to 9 times the length of the recording: about 25 minutes for a 3-minute fight;
- longer if other recordings are ahead of yours. The page shows the progress; you don't have to keep it open.

### Privacy

- Recordings are stored in Google Cloud in the United States.
- Your recordings and reports are visible only to you and the administrator (the project's author, to look into
  problems); other testers cannot see them.
- When the private test ends, every tester's recordings, clips and reports are deleted.
- This is a test version: it may get things wrong, and data may be reset.

### Feedback

What helps most:
- a failure with the **wrong move** or the **wrong time**;
- a failure that was **missed**, or one that **isn't real**;
- anything on the page that is confusing.

Just reply to the person who invited you, with the recording's name and the failure's time (for example "the one at
1:58.7 was not Hawk Shot").
